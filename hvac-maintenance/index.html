The Benefits of Professional HVAC Maintenance

Homeowners can take care of a short list: changing filters on time, keeping the outdoor coil clear, leaving registers open, and glancing at the drain. The rest takes instruments, because the problems that leave you stranded give no visible warning. A capacitor that has lost 20 percent of its rating looks the same as a new one. Low charge shows up only in superheat and subcooling readings. Cracked heat exchangers, weak flame signals, marginal pressure switches, and amp draws closing in on the breaker's limit are all invisible until measured, and all of them cost less to fix before they fail.

How to Prevent HVAC Maintenance Problems

A few simple habits now, like regular filter changes and seasonal tune-ups, can add years to your system's life. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Pay attention to new noises or longer cycles and mention them at your next visit
  • Get your tune-ups on the calendar during mild weather, well before the rush
  • Flush the condensate line every spring so algae cannot build up and clog it
  • Replace your filter on a one- to three-month cycle; nothing else you do pays off more
  • Give the condenser room to breathe and rinse its coil lightly each spring
  • Resist closing vents in spare rooms; the system is designed with all registers open
  • Check carbon monoxide alarms monthly in any home with fuel-burning appliances
