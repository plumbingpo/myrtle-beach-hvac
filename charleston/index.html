When Your System Fails in Charleston

The emergency calls we run usually follow a pattern. On the cooling side, it's an AC blowing warm air, an outdoor unit that hums without starting, or an evaporator coil iced over. A little caution before we arrive can prevent a much larger repair. An iced-over AC should be turned off at the thermostat with the fan set to "on", which lets the coil thaw faster. A short-cycling furnace, or any burning smell, means it's time to shut the unit off at its service switch and call us immediately.

Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Charleston

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and schedule a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like higher energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. Replacing on your own schedule costs less and causes far less stress than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of the year.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, neglected for a season, can ruin the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number on your phone now so help is one call away the moment something seems off in your home. A quick call saves time, money, and miserable nights.
