Restaurant Services

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

Few buildings work an HVAC system harder than a restaurant. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Myrtle Beach HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we schedule service around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with written service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. You get detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

Equipment replacements and maintenance schedules are planned with owners, managers, and kitchen staff, so everything fits the hours you're open. When a job can't happen during service, we do it after close, keeping the kitchen running and guests comfortable throughout.

Safe Installation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Myrtle Beach HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.
