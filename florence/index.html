Keeping Your Florence HVAC System Running Longer

Routine maintenance makes all the difference between a system that lasts and one that breaks down in the middle of a heat wave. Our advice for homeowners is a short list: fresh filters every one to three months, at least two feet of open space around the outdoor condenser, a gentle rinse each spring for the condenser coil, and a professional tune-up every spring and fall, so problems are found while they're still inexpensive.

The age of your equipment plays a big role. With regular service, central air usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts showing warning signs, like higher energy bills, rooms that won't reach the setpoint, or repeat service calls, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement is cheaper and is far less stressful than scrambling for a new system during the first heat wave of the year.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.
