Clear Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Nobody likes a surprise on the invoice. So Myrtle Beach HVAC prices every Myrtle Beach job upfront, after a complete diagnostic that looks at the equipment's age, fuel type, and tonnage, refrigerant levels, coil and heat exchanger condition, and the ductwork feeding each room. You'll know what's wrong, what it takes to fix, and exactly what it will cost before you say yes.

A lot of HVAC failures give quiet warnings long before the system quits. Watch for rising utility bills, weak airflow at the registers, frost on the copper lines, or a bedroom that never catches up to the thermostat. Behind those symptoms are usually slow refrigerant leaks, worn blower bearings, or a capacitor on its way out. An early service call at the first sign often costs far less than an emergency replacement.

Not every visit is about a breakdown. Many Myrtle Beach homeowners call us to evaluate an aging system before it fails, and it's a smart move. With a typical AC or furnace lifespan of 12 to 18 years, a replacement on your own timeline beats scrambling for a new unit during a cold snap. We'll give you a straight answer on how much life your equipment has left.

Maintenance Plans

Seasonal HVAC Maintenance Plans

Preventing a breakdown beats paying for one. That's the idea behind our seasonal plans. On each scheduled visit, we clean coils and burners, confirm the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all tailored to the equipment in your home.

Every maintenance plan we write is a little different. An older furnace needs a closer look than a brand-new heat pump, and a dusty, high-pollen area means condenser coils clog faster. We set up spring cooling and fall heating visits around your equipment and climate, so every tune-up keeps the system efficient and protects the equipment you've already paid for.

Consider maintenance as protection for equipment you've already paid for. Each tune-up helps keep the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most households, a plan costs far less than replacing the system ahead of schedule.

Sized for Your Home

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

When it comes to HVAC, bigger is not better. An oversized unit turns on and off too often, wastes energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs nonstop and still can't keep up on the hottest days. That's why we never just copy the tonnage of the old unit. We run a real load calculation that factors in your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

We walk you through the options for most homes, especially growing families with rising comfort demands. A single-stage system that served a small household rarely satisfies a finished basement and two added bedrooms, and a modern two-stage or variable-speed heat pump can hold tighter temperatures while cutting energy use dramatically. If you're weighing furnace-and-AC against a heat pump, or ducted against ductless, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: SEER2 efficiency ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting requirements, and upfront installation work, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Frequent Failures

The HVAC Repairs Myrtle Beach Homes Need Most

Most service calls we get in Myrtle Beach come down to a handful of familiar problems. The air conditioner blows warm air. The furnace won't light, or it lights and shuts off minutes later. The evaporator coil freezes solid, the outdoor unit hums but never kicks on, or a ductless head flashes an error code. Add refrigerant leaks, clogged drains, and forgetful thermostats, and you have the full list. We fix every one of them, always targeting the component that actually failed.

Not every HVAC problem is equally urgent. A weak capacitor only keeps the compressor from starting. A cracked heat exchanger, on the other hand, can let combustion gases into the house, and a backed-up condensate drain can soak through a ceiling before anyone notices. Our technicians carry the parts to swap capacitors, contactors, fan motors, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards, seal ductwork that's leaking air into the attic, and recharge refrigerant once a leak has been repaired.
